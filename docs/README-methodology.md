## Governing Methodological Sequence

> **Corpus Integrity → Corpus Linguistic & Deontic Analysis → Policy Representation Discovery → Obligation Extraction → Validation → Semantic Policy Intelligence**

## Standing Principles

- Let the corpus teach us the data model.
- Quality thresholds are evidence-derived, not invented.
- Preserve source evidence separately from interpretation.
- Responsible party is a semantic relation, not merely an entity mention or grammatical subject.
- Hedging, prohibition, conditions, exceptions, temporal constraints, and inherited context can be material policy meaning.
- False negatives are a first-order risk.
- Every consequential cross-corpus finding is pre-decisional and requires policy-owner review.
- The repository represents a frozen research snapshot captured 2026-08-16 and is not an authoritative live source of USDA policy.
