                                        CHAPTER 2
                                   GENERAL INFORMATION


      1         PURPOSE

                This Departmental Manual establishes policy and procedures
                for physical security standards within Information Technology (IT)
                Restricted Space. This refers to special use space in USDA facilities
                that house web farms, computer or telecommunications
                equipment/devices and the general security employed
                surrounding these areas. This policy and guidance represents
                minimum physical security standards for all IT Restricted Space in
                USDA. Cyber Security (CS) does not have direct responsibility for
                the control of physical security in USDA facilities. However, CS is
                responsible for security of the critical IT Infrastructure resident in
                these facilities. In order to meet the regulatory requirements for the
                infrastructure outlined below, we are publishing this policy and
                guidance.

                Part 1 identifies procedures for controlling and securing
                all IT Restricted Space, which can include by are not limited to,
                Computing Facilities, Web Farms, Telecommunications/Magnetic
                Media Rooms, Sensitive Compartmented Information Facilities
                (SCIF), and Isolation Zones. It also establishes a requirement for
                formal coordination with Cyber Security concerning the design
                and construction of all new IT Restricted Space in USDA.


      2         CANCELLATION

                This Departmental Manual will be in effect until superseded.
