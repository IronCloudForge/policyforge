    DELEGATION OF MIGRATORY BIRD CONSERVATION COMMISSION
                         AUTHORITY


1. PURPOSE

  This Secretary Memorandum delegates authority to the Chief of the Natural Resources
  Conservation Service (Chief) to serve as the Secretary's proxy to the Migratory Bird
  Conservation Commission.

  The Migratory Bird Conservation Act, 16 U.S.C. §§ 715 - 715s, authorizes the Secretary
  of the Interior to conduct investigations and publish documents related to North American
  birds and establishes a Migratory Bird Conservation Commission (MBCC) to approve
  areas recommended by the Secretary of the Interior for acquisition. The MBCC also
  approves wetlands conservation projects recommended by the North American Wetlands
  Conservation Council under the North American Wetlands Conservation Act, 16 U.S.C. §§
  4401-14.

  The MBCC was established to "consider and pass upon any area of land, water, or land
  and water that may be recommended by the Secretary of the Interior for purchase or rental
  under this subchapter." 16 U.S.C. § 715a. The MBCC is authorized to locate, survey, and
  acquire suitable areas of land and water for use as migratory bird reservations and to
  develop and maintain those areas under the administration of the Secretary of the Interior.
  16 U.S.C. § 715k. In addition, the Act authorizes the MBCC to conduct investigations and
  produce publications relating to North American birds. Id.


2. DELEGATIONS

  I hereby delegate to the Chief authority vested in the Secretary of Agriculture to represent
  the Department and serve as the Secretary's proxy to the Migratory Bird Conservation
  Commission.
3. EXISTING DIRECTIVES

  Prior delegations of authority, administrative regulations, and other directives not
  inconsistent with the provisions of this Memorandum will remain in full force and effect.


4. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TERMINATION

  This Memorandum is effective immediately and will remain in effect until January 20,
  2029.
