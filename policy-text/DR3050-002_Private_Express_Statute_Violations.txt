                                                                      Division, OO

1      PURPOSE

                       This regulation is to inform all agencies and staff offices of their responsibility
              to ensure that private carriers are not used to transport items in violation of the Private
              Express Statutes. These statutes restrict the transportation of certain items by means other
              than the U.S. Postal Service (USPS).




2      POLICY

                        It is the policy of USDA to comply with the provisions of the Private Express
              Statutes (39 U.S.C., 601-606 and 39 CFR parts 310 & 320) when sending material
              delivered by persons other than USDA employees.




3      SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS

                        Effective October 1, 1983, the Director of Transportation, General Services
              Administration, entered into a contract with DHL Airways, Inc., (#GS 00T 42276)
              whereby overnight service is offered to Federal Agencies for express small package
              shipments. While use of this service is generally mandatory, employees should be
              cautioned regarding the shipment of recording discs, magnetic tapes and messages or
              letters through DHL and other private carriers. In most cases, it is unlawful under the
              Private Express Statutes for any one other than USPS to carry such items.
