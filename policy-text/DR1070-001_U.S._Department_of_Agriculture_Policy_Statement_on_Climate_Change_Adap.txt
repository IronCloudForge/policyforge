 8   1. PURPOSE
 9
10     This Departmental Regulation (DR) provides guidance on the establishment and periodic
11     revision of the United States Department of Agriculture’s (USDA) Climate Change
12     Adaptation Plan.
13
14     Climate change poses a significant risk to the agriculture and forestry sectors and the
15     communities that support and depend upon them. Through climate adaptation planning and
16     implementation, USDA will identify how climate change is likely to affect its ability to
17     achieve its mission, operations, and policy and program objectives. Climate change
18     adaptation is a critical complement to mitigation; both are required to address the causes and
19     consequences of climate change. Through climate adaptation planning, USDA will develop,
20     prioritize, implement, and evaluate actions to minimize climate risks, and exploit new
21     opportunities that climate change may bring. By integrating climate change adaptation
22     strategies into USDA’s programs and operations, USDA better ensures that taxpayer
23     resources are invested wisely, and that USDA services and operations remain effective under
24     current and future climate conditions. Through climate adaptation planning, USDA is taking
25     a leadership role in ensuring the vision of a resilient, healthy, and prosperous Nation in the
26     face of a changing climate.

29   2. ACTIONS ORDERED
30
31     This policy establishes the USDA directive to integrate climate change adaptation planning,
32     implementing actions, and performance metrics into USDA programs, policies, and
33     operations in accordance with executive orders and additional guidance from CEQ.
34
35     a.   The Chief Economist, with the full support and participation of USDA Mission Areas,
36          agencies, and staff offices, will:
37
38          (1) Develop a USDA Climate Change Adaptation Plan in accordance with
39              Departmental and Inter-agency guidance;
40
 1        (2) Issue guidance to Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices to complete or update
 2            their climate adaptation plans, as well as required interim deliverables; and
 3
 4        (3) Update the USDA Climate Change Adaptation Plan as appropriate and provide
 5            progress reports on the status of implementation efforts annually.
 6
 7   b.   USDA Mission Area, agency, and staff office heads, in developing organization-specific
 8        contributions, will:
 9
10        (1) Analyze how climate change may affect the ability of their organization to achieve
11            its mission and policy, program, and operational objectives and authorities to:
12
13            (a) Identify potential impacts of climate change on their organization’s areas of
14                responsibility;
15
16            (b) Prioritize, implement, and integrate response actions into their Mission Area’s,
17                agency’s, or staff office’s operation, contingent on the availability of resources;
18
19            (c) Continuously assess and improve the capacity to adapt to current and future
20                changes in the climate; and
21
22            (d) Prepare contributions to the Department’s Climate Change Adaptation Plan.
23
24        (2) Identify, as appropriate, key performance measures to evaluate progress in climate
25            change adaptation in the annual Departmental and Mission Area, agency, and staff
26            office budget material, to include measures in the Summary of Budget and
27            Performance section of the explanatory notes, submitted as part of the
28            Congressional justification.
29
30            (a) Identify, to the extent possible, the costs associated with the accomplishment of
31                Mission Area, agency, or staff office performance measures and provide
32                accessible information to producers; and
33
34            (b) Identify returns to Mission Area, agency, or staff office end-users for climate
35                adaptation actions in terms of a list of expected accomplishments.
36
37        (3) Identify, as part of the annual budget process, to the Office of Budget and Program
38            Analysis (OBPA), areas where budget adjustments would be necessary to carry out
39            actions identified under this DR.
40
41        (4) Identify, as appropriate, for USDA’s Office of the General Counsel, areas where
42            legal analysis is needed to carry out actions identified under this DR; and
43
44        (5) Identify the point of contact and coordinate actions with USDA’s Global Change
45            Task Force, as appropriate.
