      1. PURPOSE

         The purpose of USDA’s Cross Training Program (CTP) is to educate USDA employees in
         Farm Service Agency (FSA), Natural Resource Conservation Service (NRCS) and Rural
         Development (RD) service centers on the full range of grants, loans, services and programs
         administered by the Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS), FSA, NRCS, the Risk
         Management Agency (RMA) and RD. The overall goal of CTP is to improve the level of
         customer service provided.


      2. AUTHORITIES

            related guidance issued by the Office of Personnel Management;

         b. Title 5, Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), Part 410, Training, Section 305,

4. BACKGROUND

  USDA’s mission is to provide leadership on food, agriculture, natural resources, rural
  development, nutrition and related issues based on sound public policy, the best available
  science, and efficient management. How well our customers are served, the level of
  customer satisfaction, and our delivery processes are essential to accomplishing our
  mission. Often, customers are not certain about the specific USDA programs that are
  available to meet their needs.

  Historically, USDA employees have been educated only on the programs in their area of
  specific responsibility and are often not familiar with programs offered by other USDA
  agencies that would help a customer further. Employees in the FSA, NRCS, and RD who
  interact with the public in service centers should be able to share information about other
  USDA programs so that a customer’s needs are met.


5. SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS

  This is a new policy.


6. POLICY

  a. To improve customer service, all employees in the FSA, NRCS, and RD service centers
     who interact with the public are required to complete all courses in the CTP curriculum.

  b. Supervisors in the FSA, NRCS, and RD are encouraged to build inter-agency program
     knowledge of their employees who communicate with the public by ensuring those
     employees complete the required cross training as part of employees’ individual
     development plans.
  c. For existing employees, all of the training listed in Appendix I must be completed by
     the end of calendar year 2013. Any future cross training courses new to the employee in
     AgLearn, must be completed by the end of the third quarter of the fiscal year annually.
     New employees to whom this training requirement applies must complete all available
     CTP courses within their first 6 months on duty.

  d. The training is developed and maintained by a working group that is led by the USDA
     Virtual University with members from FSA, NRCS, RD, and the Office of Advocacy
     and Outreach (OAO).


7. RESPONSIBILITIES

                                            2
a. The USDA Virtual University:

   (1)   Works with representatives from FSA, NRCS, RD, and OAO to lend training
         expertise and ensure that training coursework is designed to promote adult
         learning.

   (2)   Issues a call for a report of employees who have completed the training.

   (3)   Ensures that the curriculum is evaluated quarterly and is up to date.

   (4)   Convenes training and subject matter experts from FSA, NRCS, RD, and OAO to
         update courses as needed, for example based on changes resulting from new
         legislation making changes to programs.

b. The USDA’s OAO shares the training links with the following groups to ensure that
   they are aware of the full range of USDA’s grants, loans and services that are available
   to the communities they represent.

   (1)   Liaisons of the 1890 Program. The mission of the 1890 program is to attract
         students into careers in agriculture and related fields; share expertise and
         resources in areas such as agricultural research, extension and teaching programs,
         technology development, limited-resource farmers and minority farmer programs,
         and rural development programs; and to increase the involvement of the 1890
         Land-Grant Institutions and Tuskegee University in the delivery of USDA
         programs.

   (2)   Liaisons of the 1994 Program. The mission of this program is to provide grant
         funds and encourage applications for the Equity in Educational Land-Grant Status
         Act of 1994 (Tribal Colleges Extension Services) Program. This Program
         provides funding for the 1994 Land-Grant Institutions to conduct non-traditional
         education and outreach activities to help meet the needs of the Native American
         people, and to provide essential services to their communities.

   (3)   Liaisons to the Hispanic Serving Institutions (HSIs). The mission of the Hispanic
         Serving Institutions program is to maintain strategic partnerships between USDA
         and HSIs that provide equitable access to employment, educational and
         institutional development opportunities.

   (4)   2501 [Outreach and Assistance for Socially Disadvantaged Farmers and Ranchers
         (OASDFR) Competitive Grants Program] Community Based Organization
         recipients.

c. Supervisors in the FSA, NRCS, and RD ensure that appropriate employees complete
   the training and report it to the Virtual University as requested.

d. The Office of the Chief Information Officer’s Team AgLearn ensures that training
   courses are accessible to employees and members of Community Based Organizations,
   including Liaisons from the 1890, 1994, and HSI programs, in AgLearn.

e. Agencies other than the AMS, FSA, NRCS, RMA and RD may be required to share or
   develop course content and participate in the CTP as needs arise based on new
   legislation related to USDA’s grants, loans, and programs for customers.
