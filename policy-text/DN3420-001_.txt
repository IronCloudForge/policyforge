1. PURPOSE 
This Departmental Notice (DN) guides all United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) 
Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices on updating cybersecurity Departmental directives 
and their local procedures. This DN requires Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices to 
update their policy documents to align with the most recent release of the related 
authoritative references, as directed by Departmental Regulation (DR) 0100-001, 
Departmental Directives System. These authorities include enacted legislation or rules in the 
Federal Register, Executive Orders, Office of Management and Budget (OMB) and 
Department of Homeland Security (DHS) cybersecurity directives, or guidance from the 
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). 

2. SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS AND CANCELLATIONS 


a. This DN is effective immediately upon issuance and will expire within 1 year of that 
date. 




b. The requirements of this DN will be codified in the forthcoming policy DR, Risk 
Management Framework. 





3. SCOPE 


This DN applies to all: 


a. USDA Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices; 
b. Personnel working for, or on behalf of, the USDA who hold information security and 
privacy duties that fall under the purview of the authoritative references, noted in 
Section 1; and 
c. USDA published cybersecurity and privacy policies and procedures. 




4. POLICY 


a. All policies and procedures from USDA Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices will 
comply with respective authoritative references within the prescribed timeframes and as 
directed by DR 0100-001, Departmental Directives System: 




(1) Per Section 9c(2), determine the impact of new and revised laws, regulations, 
standards, and guidance, and issue or update Departmental directives as appropriate 
and; 






(2) Per Section 9c(12), at least annually, review Departmental directives for which they 
are responsible and amend, revise and reissue, rescind, or codify as appropriate. 


b. Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices will conduct a gap analysis of current policies 
and procedures against any changes in respective authoritative references and act upon 
gaps in accordance with DR 0100-001: 




(1) Per Section 9b(2), ensure that all Departmental directives in their area of 
responsibility are complete, accurate, and current; 






(2) Per Section 9c(1), prepare Departmental directives within the scope of their 
delegated Authority; and 






(3) Comply with Sections 9c(2) and 9c(12). 






This analysis will guide and inform updates to policy and procedures. 

c. Authoritative references include but are not limited to enacted legislation or rules in the 
Federal Register, Executive Orders, and guidance from OMB, DHS, or NIST (see 
Appendix B, Authorities and References). 





d. Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices will document and analyze lessons learned on 
their programs, control activities, procedures, and tasks. They will use this qualitative 
and quantitative data to assess effectiveness and to guide process improvement. 




5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES 


a. The USDA Chief Information Officer (CIO) will: 


(1) Ensure USDA complies with Federal laws, regulations, and directives related to 
information systems security; and 
(2) Oversee the information security and privacy program and control activities, to 
ensure they are implemented, operating as intended, and producing the desired 
outcomes to meet all requirements. 






b. The USDA Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) will implement and manage the 
Departmentwide Cybersecurity Program. 

6. INQUIRIES 

Address any inquiries about this DN to the Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO), 
Information Security Center (ISC) via SMD-PCB-Policy@usda.gov.
