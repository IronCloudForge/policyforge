                      SECRETARY'S MEMORANDUM 1077-011

                                      March 13, 2024

                 Delegation of Authority for the Under Secretary for
                         Farm Production and Conservation
               Under Section 607 of the Rural Development Act of 1972



1. PURPOSE

  The purpose of this memorandum is to delegate authority to the Under Secretary for the
  Farm Production and Conservation (FPAC) Mission Area to enter into cooperative
  agreements with Federal agencies, State and local governments, and any other
  organization or individual related to the financial and technical assistance required to
  render support to FPAC agencies and programs.


2. DELEGATIONS

  a.   The Under Secretary for FPAC is hereby delegated the authority vested in the
       Secretary under Section 607(b)(4) of the Rural Development Act of 1972 (7 United
       States Code (U.S.C.) 2204b(b)(4)) to enter into cooperative agreements with Federal
       agencies, State and local governments, and any other organization or individual
       related to financial and technical assistance.

  b.   This delegation allows the Under Secretary for FPAC to enter into cooperative
       agreements to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of Federal programs that
       affect rural areas under the FPAC Mission Area.


3. EXISTING DIRECTIVES

  Prior delegations of authority, administrative regulations, and other directives not
  inconsistent with the provisions of this Memorandum will remain in full force and effect.


4. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TERMINATION

  This delegation is effective immediately and will remain in effect for 1 year from
  the effective date, or until such earlier time as the Department’s published
delegations of authority have been revised to incorporate the provisions of this
Memorandum.
