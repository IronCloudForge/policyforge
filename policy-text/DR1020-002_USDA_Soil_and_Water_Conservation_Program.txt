    1   PURPOSE

        This regulation assigns responsibility for administering the Soil and Water Resources
        Conservation Act of 1977 (Public Law 95-192, 16 U.S.C. 2000 et seq.).




2       SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS/CANCELLATIONS

        This regulation supersedes Secretary's Memorandum 1930, dated December 30, 1977, which is
        canceled.




3       POLICY

        The Assistant Secretary for Natural Resources and Environment has Departmental leadership for
        carrying out the provisions of the Soil and Water Resources Conservation Act of 1977.




4       LEGISLATIVE REQUIREMENTS

        The Act requires the Secretary to appraise on a continuing basis the status, condition, and trends of
        the Nation's soil, water, and related resources. It also requires the Secretary to develop a national
        soil and water conservation program and to report annually to the Congress on progress in
        implementing the program.




5       AUTHORITY

        The Assistant Secretary for Natural Resources and Environment may use the agencies of the
        Department as necessary to carry out the Department's functions under this Act.
