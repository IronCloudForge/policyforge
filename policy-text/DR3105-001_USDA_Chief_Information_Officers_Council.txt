1. PURPOSE

     This Departmental Regulation (DR) maintains the United States Department of Agriculture
     (USDA) Chief Information Officers (CIO) Council as a permanently chartered committee.
     This DR defines the CIO Council’s scope, authority, membership, and voting guidelines.


2. SCOPE

     This policy applies to all USDA Mission Areas, agencies, staff offices, employees, and
     contractors working for, or on behalf of, USDA.


3. SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS/CANCELLATIONS

     a.   This DR supersedes DR 3105-001, USDA Chief Information Officers Council, dated
          April 6, 2016.
  b.   This DR is effective as of the publication date. It will remain in effect until superseded
       or expiration.

  c.   Nothing in this DR supplants the delegation of authorities and responsibilities of the
       USDA CIO under 7 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) § 2.32, Chief Information
       Officer. Also, nothing in this DR supplants the delegation of authorities and
       responsibilities of the USDA CIO under any other authorities otherwise provided by
       law, Federal regulation, or USDA Departmental directive.

  d.   The CIO Council’s bylaws set out the operating procedures of the CIO Council. A
       current copy of these bylaws, Bylaws of the United States Department of Agriculture
       Chief Information Officers Council, appears on the CIO Council’s shared site.


4. BACKGROUND

  This DR maintains the USDA CIO Council under the authority of:

  a.   7 CFR § 2.32;

  b.   The Clinger-Cohen Act of 1996, 40 United States Code (U.S.C.) § 11101 et seq.;

  c.   Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Circular A-130, Revised, Managing
       Information as a Strategic Resource;

  d.   The Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, 44 U.S.C. § 3501 et seq.; and

  e.   IT policies the USDA CIO deems relevant to the CIO Council.


5. POLICY

  a.   The CIO Council serves as one of the principal USDA IT advisory forums of the USDA
       CIO. Its purpose is to improve the operational efficiency and strategic use of IT across
       the USDA enterprise.

  b.   The CIO Council advises the USDA CIO on the technical and managerial soundness of
       the Department’s IT initiatives, policies, procedures, and standards.

  c.   The CIO Council identifies opportunities to share information resources. It assesses the
       needs of the Department’s IT workforce. It serves as a focal point for coordinating
       responses to Departmentwide IT challenges. It partners with other governmental
       councils to address issues and risks that require multidisciplinary and multilevel
       solutions.

  d.   The CIO Council advises the USDA CIO on IT matters that may become IT investments,
       as defined by DR 3130-013, Information Technology Capital Planning and Investment
       Control. IT initiatives that the CIO Council designates as enterprise initiatives will
       remain subject to, and will conform to, DR 3130-010, United States Department of
       Agriculture Enterprise Information Technology Governance, throughout their life cycle.


6. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

  a.   The USDA CIO will:

       (1) Preside over the CIO Council;

       (2) Appoint the OCIO Executive Secretariat; and

       (3) Serve as a voting member.

  b.   The Deputy CIO will serve as a CIO Council voting member.

  c.   Mission Area Assistant CIOs will serve as CIO Council voting members.

  d.   The Director of IT, Office of the Inspector General, will serve as a CIO Council voting
       member.

  e.   Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO) Associate CIOs (ACIO) will serve as
       CIO Council advisory (non-voting) members.

  f.   OCIO Division Officers will serve as CIO Council advisory (non-voting) members.

  g.   The OCIO Executive Secretariat, appointed by the USDA CIO, will serve as a CIO
       Council advisory (non-voting) member.


7. INQUIRIES

  USDA Mission Areas, agencies, and staff offices can direct questions and inquiries regarding
  this DR via email to sm.ocio.cio.feedback@usda.gov.
