               Delegation of Authority for the Department of Agriculture
                 Under the "Service First" Initiative, 16 U.S.C. § 8544



1. PURPOSE

  The purpose of this memorandum is to delegate authority under the "Service First"
  initiative to the Assistant Secretary for Administration at the Department of Agriculture
  (USDA) to create a co-location program for Federal offices and facilities leased by the
  USDA. This furthers the Administration's Return-to-Office initiative and promotes customer
  service and efficiency.


2. DELEGATIONS

  a.   The Assistant Secretary for Administration is hereby delegated the authority under the
       Service First Initiative; Permit_s for Multijurisdictional Trips (16 United States Code
       (U.S.C.) § 8544) to create the co-location program in Federal offices and facilities
       leased by an agency of either Department.

  b.   This delegation allows all agencies and staff offices of the USDA to use the
       "Service First" initiative to exercise the authority necessary to participate in the
       co-location program, which promotes customer service and efficiency.


3. EXISTING DIRECTIVES

  Prior delegations of authority, administrative regulations, and other directives not
  inconsistent with the provisions of this Memorandum will remain in full force and effect.


4. EFFECTIVE DA TE AND TERMINATION

  This delegation is effective immediately and will remain in effect until the
Department's published delegations of authority have been revised to incorporate the
provisions of this Memorandum or it is otherwise terminated.
